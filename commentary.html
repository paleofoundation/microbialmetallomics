How we approach a paper

A commentary identifies the paper and its findings, recognizes its strengths, examines methods and interpretation, and explains what further evidence would resolve the open questions. Observations and hypotheses are distinguished, with links to the original sources.

These are independent scholarly commentaries. A post in this section does not imply journal peer review.
