Aluminum & microbes

Light-metal exposure and intestinal ecology

Why it belongs in microbial metallomics

Aluminum belongs in a broad microbial metals guide even though it is a light metal. Its relevance here is experimental exposure biology rather than an established microbial nutrient role. [1]

What microbes do with it

  • In an eight-week oral-exposure study, aluminum altered selected bacterial abundances in mice without the significant overall diversity decline seen with copper in the same experiment. [1]

Community & host evidence

The study reported increased Odoribacter and decreased Anaerotruncus, alongside a reduction in Akkermansia shared across the tested metals. [1]

Exposure & chemical form

Interpretation requires the aluminum compound, dose, duration, and route. The cited evidence is a controlled mouse ingestion experiment. [1]

What the evidence does not establish

Individual taxon changes in mice do not establish an aluminum-specific human microbiome signature, disease causation, or a clinical detoxification strategy. [1]
